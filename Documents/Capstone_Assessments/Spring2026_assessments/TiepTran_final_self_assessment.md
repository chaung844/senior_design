For our senior design project - Matcha, I have architected the application's data flow, developed core features and designed user interface and experience. Specifically, by utilizing Vision Language Models (VLMs) via AWS Bedrock, I have built a documents processing pipeline capable of extracting structured data from receipt pdfs and images. By also integrating SQS message queues, the pipeline is capable of retrying failed jobs and ensuring every parsing jobs are handled and not get lost. I also contributed to the design of our database: user roles, account book management, statement states. Additionally, I contributed to the design and development of FastAPI endpoints and worker jobs handler to ensure scalability. On the frontend side, I have developed an interactive, role-based dashboard that centralized documents uploading, parsing, editing, matching and exporting, allowing for an intuitive user reconciliation workflow. On top of this, I utilized Shadcn UI library to designed a unified design language and branding all across the application. 

I have applied and built upon the skills identified in the initial assessment from last Fall. In details, I was able to integrate a new language model architecture - VLM - to our system's core component as proposed. In addition to this, I also learned a lot about system design and introduced a message queuing service to our system, allowing our application to be more scalable and resilient to network failures. I also learned a lot about AWS services and AI model hosting services in general (how to balance cost and performance) as we discover options for the hosting of our application and VLM. Overall, this project has allow me to move from merely designing software solution to actively thinking about how to architect a solution to serve for thousands of users reliability and intuitively. As a result, Matcha is a system that can be scaled easily with AWS serverless services. However, I think we still have a lot of work to do in regards to actually positioning Matcha as a commercial product and not just a customized solution to only our sponsor - Midea.
